Cowboy Butter Chicken Tenders – 22-Minute Dinner

Mars here. Cowboy butter — that garlicky, lemony, herb-loaded dipping butter — turned plain chicken tenders into the most requested dinner of the month at my table.

Juicy golden chicken tenders served with viral cowboy butter: a melted garlic-herb-lemon dipping sauce with a chili kick that makes everything taste better.

Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • The butter is the recipe: garlic, lemon, mustard, herbs and heat in melted butter — it makes simple seared chicken taste outrageous.
  • Tenders cook in minutes: their size means juicy results with almost no technique required.
  • One pan, two jobs: the chicken drippings flavor the butter that gets made in the same skillet.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Chicken tenders: breasts sliced into strips work identically. Thighs bring extra insurance against drying.
  • Dijon: do not skip it — it emulsifies the butter and adds the subtle sharpness people cannot place.
  • Fresh herbs: parsley is the base; thyme or chives layer it. Dried herbs sink — use fresh here.
  • Dry the tenders before seasoning so they brown instead of steam.
  • Make the butter off the heat — boiled garlic butter loses the fresh punch this sauce is famous for.
  • Serve the butter warm; it thickens as it cools (also excellent brushed on bread).

Kitchen Tools

  • Large skillet or nonstick pan
  • Tongs
  • Instant-read meat thermometer
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Sharp knife and cutting board

Cowboy Butter Chicken Tenders

Juicy golden chicken tenders served with viral cowboy butter: a melted garlic-herb-lemon dipping sauce with a chili kick that makes everything taste better.

Prep 10 min
Cook 12 min
Total 22 min
Serves 4
Calories 470 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

4 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Season the tenders with paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.

  2. 2

    Sear in olive oil over medium-high heat, 4-5 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through (165°F / 74°C). Rest on a plate.

  3. 3

    Lower the heat; melt the butter with garlic for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Off the heat, whisk in lemon juice, Dijon, chili flakes, paprika and herbs.

  5. 5

    Serve the tenders with the warm cowboy butter for dipping, or pour it straight over.

Pro Tips for Perfect Cowboy Butter Chicken Tenders

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Whisk or stir constantly once the sauce starts to thicken so it doesn't scorch on the bottom of the pan.
  • Let it rest for a few minutes before serving so the juices redistribute.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.

Variations to Try

  • Spice level: Gently warm from the chili flakes. Halve them for kids or double for heat lovers — the butter takes it well.
  • Swap the protein: shrimp or firm tofu both work well in place of chicken with a shorter cook time.
  • Dairy-free: use full-fat coconut cream in place of heavy cream for a similar richness.

Serving Suggestions

Fries, mashed potatoes or crusty bread — anything that mops butter. For more chicken wins, my Crispy Baked Chicken Wings brings the crunch, and One-Pan Honey Butter Chicken the sweet-glazed angle.

  • Steamed rice or cauliflower rice
  • A simple green salad with vinaigrette
  • Roasted or steamed vegetables
  • Crusty bread for the sauce

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 4 days.

Freezer

Freezes well in an airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

Reheat

Reheat gently in a skillet over medium-low heat, or in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is cowboy butter?

A melted compound butter with garlic, lemon, Dijon, herbs and chili — born as a steak dip, now famous for dunking everything.

Can I bake or air-fry the tenders?

Air fryer: 400°F for 9-10 minutes, flipping once. Bake: 425°F for 14-16 minutes. Make the butter on the stove either way.

Is cowboy butter spicy?

Gently warm from the chili flakes. Halve them for kids or double for heat lovers — the butter takes it well.
