Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Roasting concentrates everything: the tomatoes burst and caramelize while the feta softens into a salty cream — flavor you cannot get on a stovetop.
  • Feta is the sauce: its saltiness seasons the entire dish; you barely add anything else.
  • One baking dish: the sauce builds itself in the oven while you do something else entirely.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Feta: buy a block in brine, not crumbles — crumbles are drier and never melt as creamy.
  • Tomatoes: cherry or grape only; large tomatoes water the sauce down.
  • Pasta: ridged shapes like rigatoni grab the chunky sauce best.
  • Use a dish just big enough — tomatoes crowded snugly steam and burst rather than drying out.
  • Mash and mix while everything is hot; the sauce comes together in seconds.
  • The pasta water is not optional here — the sauce needs it to coat instead of clump.

Kitchen Tools

  • Large skillet or nonstick pan
  • Baking sheet or baking dish
  • Large pot for boiling pasta
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Sharp knife and cutting board

Baked Feta Pasta

The TikTok-famous baked feta pasta: a whole block of feta roasted with burst cherry tomatoes, garlic and olive oil, mashed into an instant creamy sauce.

Prep 5 min
Cook 35 min
Total 40 min
Serves 4
Calories 520 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

4 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss the tomatoes, garlic and most of the olive oil in a baking dish.

  2. 2

    Place the feta block in the center, drizzle with remaining oil, and season everything with chili flakes, oregano and pepper.

  3. 3

    Bake 30-35 minutes until the tomatoes burst and the feta is golden at the edges.

  4. 4

    Meanwhile, cook the pasta in salted water; reserve 1 cup pasta water.

  5. 5

    Mash the feta and tomatoes together into a creamy sauce.

  6. 6

    Fold in the hot pasta with a splash of pasta water, then the basil. Taste and season.

Pro Tips for Perfect Baked Feta Pasta

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Toss or fold gently — overmixing can break apart delicate ingredients like gnocchi or meatballs.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.

Variations to Try

  • Swap the pasta shape: any short pasta works in place of what's listed.
  • Dairy-free: use full-fat coconut cream in place of heavy cream.
  • Add protein: stir in cooked chicken, shrimp, or Italian sausage.

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days.

Freezer

Freezes well in an airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

Reheat

Reheat gently in a skillet over medium-low heat, or in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a different cheese?

Goat cheese melts even creamier; ricotta salata or boursin also work. Classic feta gives the signature salty tang.

My sauce came out watery. Why?

Big tomatoes or an oversized dish. Stick to cherry tomatoes, snug dish, and let it bake the full time so the juices concentrate.

Can I add protein?

Absolutely — fold in shredded rotisserie chicken or seared shrimp at the end, or serve it as a side to grilled meat.
