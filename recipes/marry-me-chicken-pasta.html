Marry Me Chicken Pasta – Ready in 30 Minutes

By Mars Published April 22, 2026
★★★★★ 4.9 (65 ratings)
📌 Save to Pinterest Share

Mars here. The internet named it, but my kitchen confirmed it — this marry me chicken pasta is the one-pot dinner that lives up to every bit of its hype. One bite of that creamy sun-dried tomato parmesan sauce and you’ll understand why it went viral.

Tender chicken and penne in a creamy, garlicky sun-dried tomato parmesan sauce — the viral Marry Me Chicken, turned into a true one-pot pasta dinner ready in 30 minutes.

Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • One pot, real flavor: the pasta cooks directly in the broth and chicken drippings, so the starch thickens the sauce and nothing is wasted.
  • Sun-dried tomatoes lead: their concentrated sweet-tang is the signature of the dish — no fresh tomato can replace it.
  • Cream added late: stirring it in after the pasta cooks keeps the sauce silky instead of boiled and heavy.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Chicken: breasts are traditional; thighs are even juicier and harder to overcook.
  • Sun-dried tomatoes: use the oil-packed jar, and cook the garlic in a spoonful of that oil for extra depth.
  • Parmesan: grate it fresh — pre-shredded cheese makes the sauce grainy.
  • Do not fully cook the chicken in the first sear — it finishes in the simmering pasta and stays tender.
  • Stir the pasta twice during simmering so nothing sticks, but keep the lid on between stirs.
  • If the sauce looks tight, loosen with a splash of broth before serving; it thickens fast off the heat.

Kitchen Tools

  • Large skillet or nonstick pan
  • Tongs
  • Large pot for boiling pasta
  • Instant-read meat thermometer
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Sharp knife and cutting board

Marry Me Chicken Pasta

This marry me chicken pasta is the viral one-pot dinner everyone is making — tender chicken and penne in a creamy sun-dried tomato parmesan sauce, ready in 30 minutes. Better than the original.

Prep 10 min
Cook 20 min
Total 30 min
Serves 4
Calories 620 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

4 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Season the chicken with Italian seasoning, salt and pepper.

  2. 2

    Sear the chicken in olive oil over medium-high heat until golden, about 5 minutes, then push to one side.

  3. 3

    Add garlic, sun-dried tomatoes and paprika; cook 1 minute until fragrant.

  4. 4

    Pour in the broth, scrape the bottom, and stir in the penne. Cover and simmer 12-14 minutes, stirring twice.

  5. 5

    Lower the heat, stir in cream and parmesan until silky.

  6. 6

    Fold in spinach until wilted, taste for salt, and finish with fresh basil.

Pro Tips for Perfect Marry Me Chicken Pasta

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer, not a rolling boil — high heat can cause cream-based sauces to separate.
  • Toss or fold gently — overmixing can break apart delicate ingredients like gnocchi or meatballs.
  • Add delicate greens at the very end so they wilt gently without turning mushy or gray.

Variations to Try

  • Swap the protein: shrimp or firm tofu both work well in place of chicken with a shorter cook time.
  • Dairy-free: use full-fat coconut cream in place of heavy cream for a similar richness.
  • Spice it up: add a pinch of red pepper flakes or cayenne for extra heat.

Serving Suggestions

This dish is rich and filling on its own, but these sides complete the meal perfectly:

  • Steamed rice or cauliflower rice
  • A simple green salad with vinaigrette
  • Roasted or steamed vegetables
  • Crusty bread for the sauce

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days.

Freezer

Cream-based sauces can separate when frozen. This dish is best eaten fresh or refrigerated. If freezing, store up to 2 months and stir well when reheating.

Reheat

Warm gently in a skillet over low heat, adding a splash of cream or broth to loosen the sauce. Avoid the microwave if possible — it can make the sauce break.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it called Marry Me Chicken?

The original viral recipe claimed the creamy sun-dried tomato sauce was proposal-worthy. The name stuck — and honestly, it is not far off.

Can I use a different pasta shape?

Any short shape works — rigatoni, fusilli, shells. Adjust simmer time to about 2 minutes under the package directions and test.

Can I make it lighter?

Swap the cream for half-and-half plus a teaspoon of flour cooked with the garlic. The sauce will be thinner but still delicious.
