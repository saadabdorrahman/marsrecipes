Cajun Chicken Pasta – Ready in 30 Minutes

By Mars Published May 7, 2026
★★★★★ 4.8 (61 ratings)
📌 Save to Pinterest Share

If you’re craving a pasta dish that delivers bold flavor with minimal effort, this Cajun Chicken Pasta is your answer. Golden-seared chicken breast, tender penne, and cherry tomatoes all come together in a smoky, garlicky cream sauce — and it’s on the table in just 30 minutes.

This Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta is the kind of weeknight dinner that tastes like you spent hours in the kitchen. The secret is Cajun seasoning doing all the heavy lifting — giving the chicken a gorgeous crust and transforming a simple cream sauce into something deeply flavorful. Once you try it, it goes straight into your regular rotation.

Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • One-pan sauce: The chicken juices left in the skillet after searing build the base of the cream sauce — extra flavor with zero extra effort.
  • Cajun seasoning does double duty: It coats the chicken for a smoky crust AND seasons the sauce, so every bite is perfectly spiced.
  • Ready in 30 minutes: From cold pan to table in half an hour — faster than delivery and infinitely more satisfying.
  • Perfectly balanced heat: Creamy and rich with just enough kick — not overpowering, but unmistakably Cajun.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Chicken breast: Slice thin for faster, more even cooking. Chicken thighs also work great and stay extra juicy.
  • Cajun seasoning: Use your favorite store-bought blend or make your own. If you’re salt-sensitive, choose a low-sodium version and adjust to taste.
  • Heavy cream: This is what makes the sauce luxurious. Half-and-half works in a pinch but the sauce will be thinner.
  • Penne: The tubular shape holds the cream sauce beautifully inside. Rigatoni or rotini are great alternatives.
  • Cherry tomatoes: They burst as they cook, adding fresh acidity that cuts through the richness of the cream. Sun-dried tomatoes are a delicious swap.
  • Don’t overcrowd the pan: Sear chicken in a single layer over high heat. Crowding causes steaming instead of browning — and you want that golden crust.
  • Reserve pasta water: A splash of starchy pasta water loosens the sauce and helps it cling to every piece of penne.
  • Add Parmesan off the heat: Stir in Parmesan after removing from heat to prevent the sauce from breaking. It melts in beautifully.
  • Taste before serving: Cajun blends vary in saltiness. Always taste and adjust at the end — a little extra smoked paprika goes a long way.

Kitchen Tools

  • Large skillet or nonstick pan
  • Tongs
  • Large pot for boiling pasta
  • Instant-read meat thermometer
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Sharp knife and cutting board

Cajun Chicken Pasta

Bold cajun-spiced chicken in a rich, smoky cream sauce tossed with pasta — this cajun chicken pasta is better than any restaurant version.

Prep 10 min
Cook 20 min
Total 30 min
Serves 4
Calories 620 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

4 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Cook penne pasta in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, drain.

  2. 2

    Coat chicken with Cajun seasoning. Sear in olive oil 3-4 min per side until cooked. Remove and slice.

  3. 3

    In same pan, sauté garlic 30 sec. Add cherry tomatoes 2 min. Pour in broth, scrape fond. Add cream and paprika. Simmer 3-4 min.

  4. 4

    Stir in Parmesan. Add pasta and toss — use pasta water if too thick. Return chicken, garnish with parsley.

Pro Tips for Perfect Cajun Chicken Pasta

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer, not a rolling boil — high heat can cause cream-based sauces to separate.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.

Variations to Try

  • Spice level: Absolutely. Start with 1 tablespoon of Cajun seasoning instead of 2, and skip the smoked paprika. You’ll still get all the flavor with a much milder heat level.
  • Make-ahead tip: You can cook and store it for up to 4 days. The sauce thickens as it sits, so add a splash of cream or broth when reheating to bring it back to the right consistency.
  • Swap the protein: shrimp or firm tofu both work well in place of chicken with a shorter cook time.

Serving Suggestions

This pasta is a complete meal on its own, but it pairs beautifully with a few simple sides. Crusty garlic bread is a must for soaking up every last drop of that cream sauce. A crisp green salad with a light lemon vinaigrette balances the richness perfectly. Roasted asparagus or sautéed green beans also make excellent companions — something fresh and bright to contrast the bold, spicy flavors.

  • Steamed rice or cauliflower rice
  • A simple green salad with vinaigrette
  • Roasted or steamed vegetables
  • Crusty bread for the sauce

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days.

Freezer

Cream-based sauces can separate when frozen. This dish is best eaten fresh or refrigerated. If freezing, store up to 2 months and stir well when reheating.

Reheat

Warm gently in a skillet over low heat, adding a splash of cream or broth to loosen the sauce. Avoid the microwave if possible — it can make the sauce break.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this less spicy?

Absolutely. Start with 1 tablespoon of Cajun seasoning instead of 2, and skip the smoked paprika. You’ll still get all the flavor with a much milder heat level.

Can I use a different pasta shape?

Yes! Rigatoni, rotini, fettuccine, or bow ties all work well. Choose a shape that holds sauce — avoid very thin pasta like angel hair.

Can I make this ahead of time?

You can cook and store it for up to 4 days. The sauce thickens as it sits, so add a splash of cream or broth when reheating to bring it back to the right consistency.

What if I don’t have heavy cream?

Half-and-half is the closest substitute — your sauce will be slightly thinner but still delicious. Coconut cream works surprisingly well and adds a subtle sweetness that plays nicely with the Cajun spices.
