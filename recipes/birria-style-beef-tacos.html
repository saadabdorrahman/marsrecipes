Birria-Style Beef Tacos – With Dipping Consomme

By Mars Published December 28, 2025
★★★★★ 4.9 (53 ratings)

Mars here. The taco that broke the internet, simplified for a home kitchen — tender chili-braised beef, crispy red-stained tortillas, and that dunkable consommé.

Birria-style tacos made weeknight-friendly: beef braised in a rich guajillo-tomato broth, stuffed into crisped tortillas with melty cheese, served with consommé for dipping.

Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Dried chiles, real depth: guajillo and ancho give the signature brick-red color and gentle warmth — fruity, not fiery.
  • Chuck shreds like silk: the slow braise melts its collagen into both the meat and the consommé.
  • The fat-dip crisp: dipping tortillas in the seasoned braising fat before griddling is what makes birria tacos look and taste like birria tacos.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Dried chiles: guajillo and ancho are mild and sold in most international aisles. No substitutes capture the color.
  • Beef chuck: short ribs add even more richness; brisket works too. Avoid lean cuts.
  • Cheese: Oaxaca is traditional; low-moisture mozzarella melts nearly identically.
  • Toast the soaked, blended sauce in the pot for 2 minutes before adding broth — it deepens noticeably.
  • Do not rush the braise; the beef is ready when it falls apart at a touch, not before.
  • Strain the consommé before serving — a clean, glossy red broth is half the experience.

Birria-Style Beef Tacos

Birria-style tacos made weeknight-friendly: beef braised in a rich guajillo-tomato broth, stuffed into crisped tortillas with melty cheese, served with consommé for dipping.

Prep 20 min
Cook 2 hr 30 min
Total 2 hr 50 min
Serves 6
Calories 610 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

6 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Soak the dried chiles in hot water for 15 minutes until soft.

  2. 2

    Blend the chiles with tomatoes, half the onion, garlic, cumin, oregano, cinnamon, cloves and 1 cup of broth until smooth.

  3. 3

    Season and sear the beef in a heavy pot until browned on all sides.

  4. 4

    Pour in the chile sauce, remaining broth and bay leaves. Simmer covered on low for 2.5 hours (or pressure-cook 50 minutes) until the beef shreds easily.

  5. 5

    Shred the beef; skim the red fat from the broth into a shallow bowl.

  6. 6

    Dip tortillas in the fat, fill with beef and cheese, and crisp in a hot skillet 2 minutes per side.

  7. 7

    Serve with cilantro, onion, lime and cups of the strained consommé for dipping.

Pro Tips for Perfect Birria-Style Beef Tacos

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer, not a rolling boil — high heat can cause cream-based sauces to separate.
  • Resist lifting the lid while it cooks — every peek lets out heat and adds extra cooking time.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.

Variations to Try

  • Spice level: Mild — guajillo and ancho are about color and depth, not heat. Add a chipotle to the blend if you want fire.
  • Swap the protein: ground turkey or chicken work well in place of beef.
  • Lighter version: use lean ground beef (90/10) and drain any excess fat.

Serving Suggestions

Always with cilantro, diced onion, lime, and hot consommé for dunking. Make it a beef feast with my Smash Burger Tacos on taco night, or go quick with the Korean Ground Beef Bowl midweek.

  • Warm tortillas or flatbread
  • Fluffy white rice
  • A crisp cucumber-tomato salad
  • Pickled onions or a tangy slaw

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 4 days.

Freezer

Freezes well in an airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

Reheat

Reheat gently in a skillet over medium-low heat, or in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this authentic birria?

It is a respectful home-style shortcut. Traditional birria is goat or beef with a more complex chile blend — this captures the spirit in a regular kitchen.

Can I make it in a slow cooker or Instant Pot?

Yes — slow cooker 8 hours on low; Instant Pot 50 minutes high pressure with natural release. Sear and blend first either way.

Are birria tacos spicy?

Mild — guajillo and ancho are about color and depth, not heat. Add a chipotle to the blend if you want fire.
