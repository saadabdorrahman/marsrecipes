Easy Vegan Casserole – Creamy One-Dish Dinner

By Mars Published July 7, 2026
★★★★★ 4.8 (118 ratings)
📌 Save to Pinterest Share

This vegan casserole is the cozy one-dish dinner that convinces everyone at the table — roasted vegetables and chickpeas folded into a silky garlic-cashew cream, topped with golden herbed breadcrumbs, and baked until bubbling. No dairy, no fuss, and every bit as comforting as the classic — one of those vegan casserole dish recipes that ends up on repeat all season.

This easy vegan casserole recipe uses American flavors and comes together with everyday ingredients — no special trip to a specialty store required.

Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Roasting first, baking second. Roasting the vegetables before they go into the dish concentrates their flavor and drives off the water that makes most veggie casseroles soggy.
  • Cashew cream instead of dairy. Blended soaked cashews turn into a sauce so silky nobody asks where the cheese went — and it won’t split in the oven.
  • Chickpeas make it a full meal. Protein and bite in the same spoonful, no sides required — the same trick behind our high-protein meal roundup.

Ingredients You'll Need

Cashews want a 15-minute soak in boiling water before blending — that’s what makes the sauce silky in a regular blender. Nutritional yeast brings the savory, cheesy depth; start with 3 tablespoons and adjust. Vegetables are flexible: broccoli, cauliflower, zucchini, bell pepper, and mushrooms all work — aim for about 6 cups total and keep the pieces even. The base can be short pasta, cooked rice, or cubed roasted potatoes, which also keeps it gluten-free. If you love creamy bakes, our baked feta pasta is the vegetarian cousin of this dish.

Easy Vegan Casserole

This creamy vegan casserole bakes roasted vegetables and chickpeas in a silky cashew sauce under golden breadcrumbs — a cozy one-dish dinner everyone loves.

Prep 15 min
Cook 40 min
Total 55 min
Serves 6
Calories 340 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

6 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss the vegetables with 2 tbsp olive oil, salt and pepper; roast 20 minutes until browned at the edges.

  2. 2

    Meanwhile, cook the pasta 2 minutes short of the package time; drain.

  3. 3

    Blend the drained cashews, broth, nutritional yeast, garlic, lemon juice, onion powder, salt and pepper until completely smooth, about 60 seconds.

  4. 4

    Toast the panko with 1 tbsp olive oil and the dried herbs in a skillet until golden, 2-3 minutes.

  5. 5

    Lower the oven to 400°F (200°C). In a 9x13 baking dish, combine the pasta, roasted vegetables and chickpeas; pour the sauce over and fold gently.

  6. 6

    Scatter the toasted breadcrumbs on top and bake 15-18 minutes until bubbling at the edges.

  7. 7

    Rest 10 minutes, top with fresh parsley, and serve.

Pro Tips for Perfect Easy Vegan Casserole

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Let it rest for a few minutes before serving so the juices redistribute.
  • Toss or fold gently — overmixing can break apart delicate ingredients like gnocchi or meatballs.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.

Variations to Try

  • Vegan option: Yes — assemble it up to 24 hours ahead, cover, and refrigerate unbaked. Add 5-10 extra minutes to the bake time since it goes in cold, and add the breadcrumb topping just before baking so it stays crisp.
  • Ingredient swap: Full-fat coconut milk gives the same silkiness with a hint of sweetness, and store-bought unsweetened oat cream also works. For a nut-free version, blend silken tofu with a splash of plant milk and lemon juice.
  • Gluten-free option: It can be — use certified gluten-free pasta or rice as the base and swap the breadcrumb topping for crushed gluten-free crackers or toasted seeds. Everything else is naturally gluten-free.

Serving Suggestions

A sharp green salad with lemon vinaigrette cuts the richness perfectly, and crusty bread never hurts. Planning a bigger spread? Pull ideas from our 25 dinner ideas or keep it light with a simple garlic parmesan pasta for the non-vegans at the table.

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days.

Freezer

Freezes well in an airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

Reheat

Reheat gently in a skillet over medium-low heat, or in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make this vegan casserole ahead of time?

Yes — assemble it up to 24 hours ahead, cover, and refrigerate unbaked. Add 5-10 extra minutes to the bake time since it goes in cold, and add the breadcrumb topping just before baking so it stays crisp.

What can I use instead of cashew cream?

Full-fat coconut milk gives the same silkiness with a hint of sweetness, and store-bought unsweetened oat cream also works. For a nut-free version, blend silken tofu with a splash of plant milk and lemon juice.

Is this casserole gluten-free?

It can be — use certified gluten-free pasta or rice as the base and swap the breadcrumb topping for crushed gluten-free crackers or toasted seeds. Everything else is naturally gluten-free.

How do I keep a vegan casserole from getting watery?

Salt and roast the vegetables first to drive off moisture, don't overcook the pasta before baking, and let the casserole rest 10 minutes after baking — the sauce thickens noticeably as it cools.

Can I freeze vegan casserole?

Absolutely. Freeze it baked, in portions, for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat covered at 350°F (175°C) with a splash of plant milk to revive the sauce.

What protein can I add to make it more filling?

Chickpeas are already in the base, but white beans, lentils, baked tofu cubes, or a plant-based ground all work — stir in about 1½ cups with the sauce. Each adds 10g+ protein per serving.
