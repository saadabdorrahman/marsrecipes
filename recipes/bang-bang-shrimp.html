Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Cornstarch beats flour: it fries lighter and crispier, and stays crunchy longer under the creamy sauce.
  • Buttermilk soak: a quick soak helps the coating cling and gently seasons the shrimp.
  • Sauce ratio is the secret: two parts mayo to one part sweet chili, sharpened with sriracha — the authentic bang bang balance.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Shrimp: large (21/25) hold their juiciness through frying. Thaw frozen shrimp fully and dry well.
  • Sweet chili sauce: Thai-style bottled sauce is the backbone — find it in the Asian aisle.
  • Sriracha: start with one teaspoon; the sauce should tingle, not burn.
  • Keep the oil at a steady 350°F — too cool makes greasy shrimp, too hot burns the coating before the shrimp cooks.
  • Drain on a rack, never paper towels, to keep the bottoms crisp.
  • Sauce just before serving — dressed shrimp soften within minutes.

Kitchen Tools

Crispy Bang Bang Shrimp

Crispy fried shrimp tossed in the legendary bang bang sauce — creamy, sweet and spicy in every bite. A 25-minute homemade version that beats the restaurant original.

Prep 10 min
Cook 15 min
Total 25 min
Serves 4
Calories 450 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

4 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Whisk the sauce ingredients together and set aside.

  2. 2

    Soak the shrimp in buttermilk for 5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Season the cornstarch with salt and pepper; dredge each shrimp until fully coated.

  4. 4

    Heat 1/2 inch of oil to 350°F (175°C) in a heavy pan.

  5. 5

    Fry the shrimp in batches, 2 minutes per side, until golden and crisp; drain on a rack.

  6. 6

    Toss the hot shrimp gently in the sauce and garnish. Serve immediately.

Pro Tips for Perfect Crispy Bang Bang Shrimp

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Whisk or stir constantly once the sauce starts to thicken so it doesn't scorch on the bottom of the pan.
  • Prep and measure every ingredient before you start cooking — it keeps the process smooth and stress-free.

Variations to Try

  • Oven / air-fryer method: Air fryer works well: spray the dredged shrimp with oil and cook at 400°F for 8-9 minutes, flipping once. Oven-baked loses some crunch.
  • Spice level: Mild-medium as written. The sweet chili dominates; sriracha sets the heat level, so adjust it to taste.
  • Swap the seafood: shrimp, salmon, and white fish are interchangeable in most of these recipes with minor timing adjustments.

Serving Suggestions

  • Steamed jasmine or coconut rice
  • A crisp green salad
  • Roasted asparagus or broccoli
  • Crusty bread for the sauce

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 2–3 days.

Freezer

Freezes well in an airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

Reheat

Reheat gently in a skillet over medium-low heat, or in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bake or air-fry instead of frying?

Air fryer works well: spray the dredged shrimp with oil and cook at 400°F for 8-9 minutes, flipping once. Oven-baked loses some crunch.

Is bang bang sauce spicy?

Mild-medium as written. The sweet chili dominates; sriracha sets the heat level, so adjust it to taste.

Can I prep anything ahead?

The sauce keeps a week refrigerated, and you can peel and dry the shrimp a day ahead. Dredge and fry just before serving.
