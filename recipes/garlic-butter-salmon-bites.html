Mars here. Salmon bites took over my dinner rotation the week I discovered them — maximum crispy surface, minimum cook time, and the garlic butter does the rest.

Crispy-edged salmon cubes basted in garlic butter, on the table in 10 minutes. The trending salmon bites recipe that makes weeknight fish feel like a treat.

Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Cubes mean crust: cutting salmon into bites multiplies the seared surface — the best part of any salmon dinner, in every single piece.
  • Two minutes per side: small cubes cook fast, so they stay silky inside while the outside crisps.
  • Butter goes in last: garlic butter added after the sear bastes the bites without burning the garlic.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Salmon: center-cut fillets cube evenly. Frozen salmon works thawed and dried extremely well.
  • Butter: real butter only — it is the sauce. Unsalted keeps the seasoning in your control.
  • Lemon: fresh juice at the end brightens the richness; bottled tastes flat here.
  • Dry the salmon twice — surface moisture is the difference between searing and steaming.
  • Do not crowd the pan; cook in two batches if needed.
  • Pull the pan off the heat the moment the bites feel just-firm; they carry on cooking in the hot butter.

Kitchen Tools

  • Large skillet or nonstick pan
  • Tongs
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Sharp knife and cutting board

Garlic Butter Salmon Bites

Crispy-edged salmon cubes basted in garlic butter, on the table in 10 minutes. The trending salmon bites recipe that makes weeknight fish feel like a treat.

Prep 5 min
Cook 6 min
Total 11 min
Serves 3
Calories 390 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

3 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Pat the salmon cubes very dry and toss with paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.

  2. 2

    Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

  3. 3

    Sear the salmon bites in a single layer, 2 minutes per side, without moving them between flips.

  4. 4

    Lower the heat, add butter and garlic, and baste the bites for 1 minute as it foams.

  5. 5

    Finish with lemon juice and parsley; serve immediately.

Pro Tips for Perfect Garlic Butter Salmon Bites

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.
  • Prep and measure every ingredient before you start cooking — it keeps the process smooth and stress-free.

Variations to Try

  • Swap the seafood: shrimp, salmon, and white fish are interchangeable in most of these recipes with minor timing adjustments.
  • Dairy-free: use coconut cream in place of heavy cream.
  • Spice it up: add extra chili flakes or a dash of hot sauce.

Serving Suggestions

  • Steamed jasmine or coconut rice
  • A crisp green salad
  • Roasted asparagus or broccoli
  • Crusty bread for the sauce

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 2–3 days.

Freezer

Freezes well in an airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

Reheat

Reheat gently in a skillet over medium-low heat, or in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know salmon bites are done?

They turn opaque and flake gently when pressed — about 4-5 minutes total. Overcooked salmon turns pale and chalky.

Can I use the air fryer?

Yes: 400°F (200°C) for 7-8 minutes, shaking once, then toss with the warm garlic butter after.

Skin on or off?

Off for bites — the cubes sear on all sides and skin gets in the way. Save skin-on for whole fillets.
