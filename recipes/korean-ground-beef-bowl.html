Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Ground beef takes the glaze: all those crumbly browned edges grab the sweet-soy sauce far better than slices ever could.
  • A 4-ingredient sauce: soy, honey, garlic, ginger — pantry staples that taste like takeout when they hit hot beef fat.
  • Built for meal prep: the beef reheats perfectly and the bowls assemble in minutes all week.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Ground beef: 85/15 has enough fat to carry flavor without a greasy sauce. Ground chicken or turkey work too.
  • Honey: brown sugar is the classic; honey glazes a little glossier. Either works measure-for-measure.
  • Gochugaru: Korean chili flakes give gentler, fruitier heat than regular flakes — use them if you have them.
  • Let the beef sit untouched for the first 2 minutes — browned beef beats grey beef every time.
  • Taste before adding any salt; soy sauce carries the seasoning.
  • Double the sauce if serving over plain rice — you will want extra to soak in.

Kitchen Tools

  • Large skillet or nonstick pan
  • Tongs
  • Instant-read meat thermometer

Korean Ground Beef Bowl

Sweet-savory Korean-inspired ground beef over steamed rice in 15 minutes — garlicky, gingery, glazed in soy and honey, and endlessly meal-preppable.

Prep 5 min
Cook 10 min
Total 15 min
Serves 4
Calories 480 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

4 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Brown the ground beef in sesame oil over medium-high heat, breaking it up, about 5 minutes. Drain excess fat if needed.

  2. 2

    Add garlic and ginger; cook 1 minute until fragrant.

  3. 3

    Stir together soy sauce, honey and chili flakes; pour over the beef.

  4. 4

    Simmer 2-3 minutes until the sauce glazes the meat.

  5. 5

    Serve over rice, topped with green onions and sesame seeds, with broccoli or cucumber alongside.

Pro Tips for Perfect Korean Ground Beef Bowl

  • Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer, not a rolling boil — high heat can cause cream-based sauces to separate.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.
  • Prep and measure every ingredient before you start cooking — it keeps the process smooth and stress-free.

Variations to Try

  • Swap the protein: ground turkey or chicken work well in place of beef.
  • Lighter version: use lean ground beef (90/10) and drain any excess fat.
  • Spice it up: add extra chili flakes or hot sauce for more heat.

Serving Suggestions

Korean Ground Beef Bowl pairs well with simple sides that let the main dish shine:

  • Warm tortillas or flatbread
  • Fluffy white rice
  • A crisp cucumber-tomato salad
  • Pickled onions or a tangy slaw

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 4 days.

Freezer

Freezes well in an airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

Reheat

Reheat gently in a skillet over medium-low heat, or in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this authentic Korean bulgogi?

No — it is a weeknight shortcut inspired by bulgogi flavors using ground beef. Authentic bulgogi uses marinated sliced ribeye.

Can I make it spicier?

Add gochujang a teaspoon at a time with the sauce, or finish with sriracha. The base recipe is family-mild.

What rice works best?

Short-grain white rice is classic; jasmine, brown rice or cauliflower rice all work fine.
