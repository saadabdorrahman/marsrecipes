Creamy Tuscan Gnocchi – One Pan, 20 Minutes

By Mars Published July 8, 2026
★★★★★ 4.8 (46 ratings)
📌 Save to Pinterest Share

The first time I made this, I couldn’t believe the sauce came together in the same time it took to boil the gnocchi. Tuscan gnocchi is everything — pillowy pasta, a sauce that tastes like it slow-cooked for hours, and spinach wilted in at the last second so the whole thing somehow feels balanced. Twenty minutes. One pan. I will not be taking questions.

This creamy tuscan gnocchi recipe uses Italian flavors and comes together with everyday ingredients — no special trip to a specialty store required.

Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Pan-fried gnocchi: skip the boiling step entirely — frying them straight in a skillet gives a golden crust the boiled version never achieves.
  • Fast Tuscan sauce: sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, cream and parmesan come together in 8 minutes but taste like a slow-simmered Sunday sauce.
  • One pan cleanup: gnocchi, sauce and spinach all finish in the same skillet.
  • Easy to customize: add Italian sausage, chicken or shrimp and the dish scales up perfectly.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Gnocchi: store-bought shelf-stable gnocchi works perfectly — no need to make your own. Fresh refrigerated gnocchi is also great.
  • Sun-dried tomatoes: oil-packed, drained. Use the oil to fry the gnocchi — it adds incredible depth.
  • Spinach: fresh baby spinach wilts down to almost nothing. Frozen spinach works; squeeze out all excess water first.
  • Heavy cream: essential for sauce stability. Lighter alternatives break when parmesan is added.
  • Parmesan: freshly grated only — pre-grated has additives that prevent proper melting.

Kitchen Tools

  • Large skillet or nonstick pan
  • Tongs
  • Large pot for boiling pasta
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Sharp knife and cutting board

Creamy Tuscan Gnocchi

Pillowy gnocchi smothered in a creamy Tuscan-style sauce with sun-dried tomatoes and spinach — this tuscan gnocchi is the easiest impressive dinner.

Prep 5 min
Cook 15 min
Total 20 min
Serves 4
Calories 540 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

4 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Pan-fry gnocchi in olive oil 2-3 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.

  2. 2

    Sauté garlic 30 sec in same pan. Add sun-dried tomatoes and red pepper flakes 1 min.

  3. 3

    Add cream and Italian seasoning. Simmer 3 min. Stir in Parmesan.

  4. 4

    Add spinach until wilted. Return gnocchi, toss to coat. Garnish with basil.

Pro Tips for Perfect Creamy Tuscan Gnocchi

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer, not a rolling boil — high heat can cause cream-based sauces to separate.
  • Add delicate greens at the very end so they wilt gently without turning mushy or gray.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.

Variations to Try

  • Vegetarian option: Use vegetable broth instead of chicken broth and it's fully vegetarian.
  • Swap the pasta shape: any short pasta works in place of what's listed.
  • Dairy-free: use full-fat coconut cream in place of heavy cream.

Serving Suggestions

This is a complete meal on its own, but a Caesar salad and garlic bread alongside make it a full Italian-style spread. For more creamy pasta nights, try my Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Pasta or Cajun Chicken Pasta.

  • A simple Caesar or arugula salad
  • Garlic bread
  • Roasted broccoli or green beans
  • Extra Parmesan at the table

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days.

Freezer

Cream-based sauces can separate when frozen. This dish is best eaten fresh or refrigerated. If freezing, store up to 2 months and stir well when reheating.

Reheat

Warm gently in a skillet over low heat, adding a splash of cream or broth to loosen the sauce. Avoid the microwave if possible — it can make the sauce break.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to pan-fry the gnocchi?

No — you can boil them instead. Cook 2-3 minutes until they float, drain and add to the sauce. You'll miss the golden crust but the sauce is still perfect.

Can I add protein?

Absolutely. Sear Italian sausage, chicken strips or shrimp first, remove, make the sauce, then add everything back together. All three work beautifully.

Is this recipe vegetarian?

Use vegetable broth instead of chicken broth and it's fully vegetarian.

What if I can't find shelf-stable gnocchi?

Fresh refrigerated gnocchi from the pasta aisle works identically. Homemade gnocchi is wonderful too but adds 30+ minutes to prep.

Can I use shelf-stable gnocchi?

Yes — shelf-stable, refrigerated, and frozen gnocchi all work. Shelf-stable sears especially well; add frozen gnocchi straight to the pan and cook 1–2 minutes longer per side.

How do I keep gnocchi from sticking to the pan?

Use a nonstick or well-seasoned skillet, make sure the oil is fully hot before the gnocchi goes in, and don't touch them for the first 3 minutes — they release naturally once golden.
