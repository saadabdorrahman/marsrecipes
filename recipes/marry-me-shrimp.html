Marry Me Shrimp – Ready in 20 Minutes

By Mars Published May 2, 2026
★★★★★ 4.9 (86 ratings)
📌 Save to Pinterest Share

If Marry Me Chicken Pasta is on repeat in your kitchen, wait until you try this shrimp version — same dreamy sauce, but shrimp cooks in 4 minutes flat. This is the weeknight dinner that earns a marriage proposal.

Juicy shrimp in a silky sun-dried tomato cream sauce — this 20-minute marry me shrimp will have everyone asking for seconds.

Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Shrimp cooks in minutes: 2 minutes per side in a hot pan — overcooking is the only real risk, and this recipe prevents it.
  • The sauce is the star: sun-dried tomatoes, heavy cream and parmesan create that cult-favorite blush sauce in under 5 minutes.
  • One pan, zero waste: the shrimp fond left in the pan builds the sauce flavor automatically.
  • Restaurant presentation, pantry ingredients: it looks and tastes like a $30 dish but costs a fraction of that.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Shrimp: large (21-25 count) peeled and deveined. Frozen works perfectly — just thaw completely and pat dry.
  • Sun-dried tomatoes: oil-packed, chopped. The oil they come in is liquid gold — use it to sear the shrimp.
  • Heavy cream: don’t substitute with half-and-half — the sauce will break. Full-fat coconut cream works for dairy-free.
  • Parmesan: freshly grated melts into the sauce. Pre-grated becomes grainy.
  • Fresh basil: added at the very end. Dried basil won’t give you the same bright finish.
  • Dry shrimp is non-negotiable — moisture steams instead of sears, and you lose that pink crust.
  • Pull the shrimp the moment they curl into a “C” shape. An “O” shape means overcooked.
  • Keep the heat at medium when making the sauce — a rolling boil will break the cream.
  • The sauce thickens as it cools; if serving over pasta, add a splash of pasta water to loosen it.

Kitchen Tools

  • Large skillet or nonstick pan
  • Tongs
  • Large pot for boiling pasta
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Sharp knife and cutting board

Marry Me Shrimp

Juicy shrimp in a silky sun-dried tomato cream sauce — this 20-minute marry me shrimp will have everyone asking for seconds.

Prep 5 min
Cook 15 min
Total 20 min
Serves 4
Calories 420 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

4 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Pat shrimp completely dry. Season with salt, pepper and smoked paprika.

  2. 2

    Heat sun-dried tomato oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear shrimp 2 minutes per side until pink and just curled. Remove to a plate.

  3. 3

    In the same pan over medium heat, sauté garlic and sun-dried tomatoes 1 minute. Pour in chicken broth, scraping up any fond. Add heavy cream and Parmesan. Simmer 3 minutes until slightly thickened.

  4. 4

    Return shrimp to the sauce. Add fresh basil, toss 30 seconds and serve immediately over pasta, rice or crusty bread.

Pro Tips for Perfect Marry Me Shrimp

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer, not a rolling boil — high heat can cause cream-based sauces to separate.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.

Variations to Try

  • Swap the protein: Yes — thaw overnight in the fridge or under cold running water for 5 minutes. Pat completely dry before seasoning.
  • Swap the seafood: shrimp, salmon, and white fish are interchangeable in most of these recipes with minor timing adjustments.
  • Dairy-free: use coconut cream in place of heavy cream.

Serving Suggestions

Serve over linguine, angel hair or creamy polenta to catch every drop of sauce. Garlic bread on the side is mandatory. Seafood lovers should also check out my Creamy Tuscan Shrimp and Spicy Garlic Butter Shrimp.

  • Steamed jasmine or coconut rice
  • A crisp green salad
  • Roasted asparagus or broccoli
  • Crusty bread for the sauce

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 2–3 days.

Freezer

Cream-based sauces can separate when frozen. This dish is best eaten fresh or refrigerated. If freezing, store up to 2 months and stir well when reheating.

Reheat

Warm gently in a skillet over low heat, adding a splash of cream or broth to loosen the sauce. Avoid the microwave if possible — it can make the sauce break.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen shrimp?

Yes — thaw overnight in the fridge or under cold running water for 5 minutes. Pat completely dry before seasoning.

Why is my sauce too thin?

Simmer it longer. The parmesan and cream need 4-5 minutes at a gentle simmer to thicken. Don’t rush it with high heat.

Can I make it ahead?

Make the sauce ahead and refrigerate. Sear the shrimp fresh and add to the reheated sauce — takes 10 minutes total.

What’s the difference from Marry Me Chicken?

Same sauce DNA, but shrimp cooks in 4 minutes vs 20+ minutes for chicken. Faster, lighter, and the briny sweetness of shrimp works beautifully with the creamy sauce.
