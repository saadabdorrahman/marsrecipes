Crockpot Marry Me Chicken Pasta – Easy Dinner

This crockpot marry me chicken pasta is about to become your new favorite slow cooker dinner. Juicy shredded chicken, a luscious sun-dried tomato cream sauce, tender pasta, and wilted spinach — all made with just 15 minutes of prep. It’s the kind of meal that has your family saying “yes!” before you even finish serving it.

This crockpot marry me chicken pasta recipe uses American flavors and comes together with everyday ingredients — no special trip to a specialty store required.

Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Truly hands-off: 15 minutes of prep, then let the crockpot do everything
  • One-pot wonder: Almost everything cooks in the slow cooker
  • Crowd-pleaser: Creamy, rich, and packed with flavor — even picky eaters love it
  • Perfect for meal prep: Makes 6 generous servings that reheat beautifully
  • Restaurant quality at home: Sun-dried tomatoes + Parmesan = incredible depth of flavor

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Chicken breasts: Boneless skinless — they shred perfectly after slow cooking. Thighs work great too
  • Sun-dried tomatoes: Use oil-packed for the best flavor. They’re the secret ingredient that makes it “marry me” worthy
  • Heavy cream: Creates that ultra-rich, velvety sauce. Don’t substitute with milk — it will be too thin
  • Parmesan cheese: Freshly grated melts better than pre-shredded and gives the sauce incredible depth
  • Fresh spinach: Stirred in at the end for color and a fresh bite
  • Don’t overcook the pasta. Cook it 1 minute under the package directions since it keeps softening in the warm sauce
  • Use the low setting if possible. Lower, slower cooking makes the chicken even more tender and flavorful
  • Shred the chicken finely. The more shredded, the better it absorbs the sauce
  • Let it rest before serving. 5–10 minutes on WARM allows the sauce to thicken to the perfect consistency
  • Season at the end. Parmesan is salty — always taste before adding extra salt

Kitchen Tools

  • 6-quart slow cooker
  • Two forks for shredding
  • Large pot for boiling pasta
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Sharp knife and cutting board

Crockpot Marry Me Chicken Pasta

This crockpot marry me chicken pasta is the ultimate set-it-and-forget-it dinner! Juicy shredded chicken in a creamy sun-dried tomato parmesan sauce. Ready in the slow cooker with just 15 minutes of prep.

Prep 15 min
Cook 6 hr
Total 6 hr 15 min
Serves 6
Calories 620 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

6 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Place chicken breasts in the bottom of the crockpot. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes.

  2. 2

    Add the chopped sun-dried tomatoes, minced garlic, and chicken broth over the chicken.

  3. 3

    Cover and cook on LOW for 5–6 hours or HIGH for 2.5–3 hours, until chicken is very tender and easily shredded.

  4. 4

    Remove chicken from the crockpot and shred with two forks. Return shredded chicken to the crockpot.

  5. 5

    Stir in the heavy cream and Parmesan cheese until fully melted and combined.

  6. 6

    Meanwhile, cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente. Drain well.

  7. 7

    Add the drained pasta and fresh spinach to the crockpot. Gently stir to combine.

  8. 8

    Cover and let sit on WARM for 5–10 minutes until sauce thickens and spinach wilts.

  9. 9

    Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve topped with extra Parmesan cheese and fresh basil.

Pro Tips for Perfect Crockpot Marry Me Chicken Pasta

  • Resist lifting the lid while it cooks — every peek lets out heat and adds extra cooking time.
  • Add delicate greens at the very end so they wilt gently without turning mushy or gray.
  • Watch the garlic closely once it hits the pan — it goes from fragrant to bitter in seconds.

Variations to Try

  • Swap the protein: It’s not recommended to cook frozen chicken in the slow cooker for food safety reasons — the USDA advises that slow cookers may not bring frozen meat to a safe temperature quickly enough. Thaw chicken overnight in the refrigerator before using.
  • Make-ahead tip: Yes! Cook and shred the chicken with the cream sauce, then refrigerate overnight. When ready to serve, reheat on LOW and add freshly cooked pasta and spinach.
  • Swap the protein: shrimp or firm tofu both work well in place of chicken with a shorter cook time.

Serving Suggestions

This rich pasta dish pairs well with simple sides that balance the creaminess:

  • Steamed rice or cauliflower rice
  • A simple green salad with vinaigrette
  • Roasted or steamed vegetables
  • Crusty bread for the sauce

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days.

Freezer

Cream-based sauces can separate when frozen. This dish is best eaten fresh or refrigerated. If freezing, store up to 2 months and stir well when reheating.

Reheat

Warm gently in a skillet over low heat, adding a splash of cream or broth to loosen the sauce. Avoid the microwave if possible — it can make the sauce break.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen chicken in the crockpot?

It’s not recommended to cook frozen chicken in the slow cooker for food safety reasons — the USDA advises that slow cookers may not bring frozen meat to a safe temperature quickly enough. Thaw chicken overnight in the refrigerator before using.

Can I make this ahead of time?

Yes! Cook and shred the chicken with the cream sauce, then refrigerate overnight. When ready to serve, reheat on LOW and add freshly cooked pasta and spinach.

How do I store leftovers?

Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. When reheating, add 2–3 tablespoons of chicken broth to thin the sauce — it thickens as it cools.

Can I freeze this crockpot marry me chicken pasta?

The chicken and sauce freeze well (up to 3 months), but pasta doesn’t freeze well as it becomes mushy. Freeze the sauce separately and cook fresh pasta when serving.
