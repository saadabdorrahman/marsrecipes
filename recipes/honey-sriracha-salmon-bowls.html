Why You'll Love This Recipe

  • Glaze meets hot pan: honey and sriracha caramelize around the seared salmon in 90 seconds — sticky, glossy, takeout-level coating.
  • Hot, cold, creamy, crunchy: warm salmon, cool cucumber and creamy avocado in one bowl is why this format went viral.
  • Rice does the balancing: plain steamed rice absorbs the bold glaze so nothing overwhelms.

Ingredients You'll Need

Everything here is available at any major supermarket. A few notes on what makes each ingredient work:

  • Salmon: cubes glaze better than fillets — more surface, more sticky edges.
  • Sriracha: one tablespoon is family-friendly warm; two is properly spicy.
  • Rice vinegar: that small splash keeps the glaze from being one-note sweet. Lemon juice works in a pinch.
  • Sear the salmon fully before the glaze goes in — sugar burns if it hits the pan too early.
  • Pull the glaze at thick-syrup stage; it keeps tightening off the heat.
  • Prep the toppings while the rice cooks and the whole meal lands in 20 minutes.

Kitchen Tools

  • Large skillet or nonstick pan
  • Tongs
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Sharp knife and cutting board

Honey Sriracha Salmon Bowls

Caramelized honey-sriracha salmon over rice with crunchy cucumber and creamy avocado — the trending salmon bowl that tastes like a restaurant lunch in 20 minutes.

Prep 10 min
Cook 10 min
Total 20 min
Serves 3
Calories 530 kcal
📌 Save to Pinterest

Ingredients

3 servings

Instructions

  1. 1

    Whisk the glaze ingredients together.

  2. 2

    Season the salmon cubes and sear in oil over medium-high, 2 minutes per side.

  3. 3

    Pour the glaze over the salmon and simmer 1-2 minutes until it caramelizes and clings.

  4. 4

    Build bowls: rice, glazed salmon, cucumber, avocado.

  5. 5

    Top with green onions, sesame seeds and a spicy mayo drizzle if you like.

Pro Tips for Perfect Honey Sriracha Salmon Bowls

  • Don't overcrowd the pan when searing — work in batches so everything browns instead of steaming.
  • Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer, not a rolling boil — high heat can cause cream-based sauces to separate.
  • Whisk or stir constantly once the sauce starts to thicken so it doesn't scorch on the bottom of the pan.

Variations to Try

  • Spice level: You control it entirely with the sriracha. One tablespoon is mild warmth; the honey rounds off the edges.
  • Ingredient swap: Edamame, shredded carrots or a soft-boiled egg keep the creamy-fresh contrast going.
  • Swap the seafood: shrimp, salmon, and white fish are interchangeable in most of these recipes with minor timing adjustments.

Serving Suggestions

Honey Sriracha Salmon Bowls pairs well with simple sides that let the main dish shine:

  • Steamed jasmine or coconut rice
  • A crisp green salad
  • Roasted asparagus or broccoli
  • Crusty bread for the sauce

Storage & Reheating

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container for up to 2–3 days.

Freezer

Freezes well in an airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.

Reheat

Reheat gently in a skillet over medium-low heat, or in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bake the salmon instead?

Yes — roast cubes at 425°F (220°C) for 8 minutes, then toss with the glaze simmered separately for 2 minutes.

How spicy are these bowls?

You control it entirely with the sriracha. One tablespoon is mild warmth; the honey rounds off the edges.

What can I swap for avocado?

Edamame, shredded carrots or a soft-boiled egg keep the creamy-fresh contrast going.
